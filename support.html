Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make a deposit?

Go to the Deposit page, enter the amount, and choose your preferred payment method. Your wallet will be credited automatically after confirmation.

When can I withdraw?

Withdrawal days depend on your VIP tier. M1-M2: Monday, M3-M4: Tuesday, M5: Wednesday, M6: Thursday, M7: Friday. Time: 10am - 6pm WAT.

How do I upgrade my VIP tier?

Go to the VIP page and select your desired tier. Complete the payment to enjoy higher benefits and withdrawal limits.

What are Mystery Boxes?

Mystery Boxes are daily rewards you can claim based on your VIP tier. Each box contains random cash rewards added directly to your wallet.
