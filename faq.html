Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about RMS.

How do the Daily Mystery Boxes work?
Once you upgrade to an M-Tier (M1 to M7), you unlock a specific number of Mystery Boxes every day. Simply tap a box to open it and instantly claim your cash reward. The amount per box depends on your tier.
When can I withdraw my funds?
Withdrawals are strictly scheduled by tier to ensure fast processing:
• M1 & M2: Monday
• M3 & M4: Tuesday
• M5: Wednesday
• M6: Thursday
• M7: Friday.
Withdrawals are only processed between 10:00 AM and 6:00 PM WAT. No withdrawals on weekends.
What is the Wealth Center?
The Wealth Center allows you to lock up funds for a fixed period (e.g., 5 days) to earn guaranteed high returns. Once the plan matures, you can claim the total amount (Principal + Profit) directly to your wallet.
How do I get Lucky Wheel spins?
You earn exactly 1 spin for every "Active Downline" you have. An active downline is a direct referral who has made at least one approved deposit. Spins reset daily!
Is there a limit on how many times I can withdraw?
Yes. To maintain platform stability, each member is allowed exactly ONE withdrawal request per day, strictly on their designated tier day.
How do I upgrade my VIP Tier?
Go to the Deposit page, fund your wallet, and then navigate to the VIP page. You can use your wallet balance to instantly upgrade to your desired M-Tier.
